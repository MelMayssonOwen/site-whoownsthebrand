Who owns Burt's Bees?

Verified to public record

Burt's Bees is owned by The Clorox Company (NYSE: CLX). Clorox acquired Burt's Bees, Inc. in late 2007 for about US$925 million, buying it from private-equity owner AEA Investors. Burt's Bees has operated as a Clorox subsidiary ever since; its U.S. trademarks are still registered to the Burt's Bees, Inc. operating entity.

Verify this ownership yourself (free)

You do not have to take our word for it. The ownership of Burt's Bees is a matter of public record. Check it in minutes:

  1. Trademark owner, search USPTO Trademark Search for “Burt's Bees”, then open the record in TSDR to see the current registered owner.
  2. Ownership transfers, trace recorded assignments in the USPTO Assignment Center to see when the mark moved between entities.
  3. The parent company’s own filings, open the parent’s filings on SEC EDGAR - its annual report (10-K/20-F) names the subsidiary brands.
  4. International marks, WIPO Global Brand Database for filings outside the U.S.
  5. Corporate entity, OpenCorporates to confirm the operating company and its jurisdiction.

Burt's Bees ownership FAQ

Who owns Burt's Bees?

The Clorox Company (NYSE: CLX) has owned Burt's Bees since November 2007, when it bought the brand for roughly US$925 million. Clorox names Burt's Bees among its brands in its annual 10-K filings.

Is Burt's Bees a natural/independent company?

It markets itself on natural ingredients, but it is a wholly owned subsidiary of Clorox, a large publicly traded consumer-goods company. The founders exited years before Clorox bought it.

How can I verify who owns the Burt's Bees trademark?

Search 'Burt's Bees' in USPTO TSDR to see the current registered owner, then open the USPTO Assignment Center to trace any recorded transfers of the registration.
