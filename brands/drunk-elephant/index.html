Who owns Drunk Elephant?

Verified to public record

Drunk Elephant is owned by Shiseido (Tokyo Stock Exchange: 4911). The Japanese cosmetics group acquired Drunk Elephant LLC in October 2019 for US$845 million. Founder Tiffany Masterson stayed on as Chief Creative Officer after the sale. Drunk Elephant's trademark rights are held under Shiseido's structure.

The ownership chain

Verify this ownership yourself (free)

You do not have to take our word for it. The ownership of Drunk Elephant is a matter of public record. Check it in minutes:

  1. Trademark owner — search USPTO Trademark Search for “Drunk Elephant”, then open the record in TSDR to see the current registered owner.
  2. Ownership transfers — trace recorded assignments in the USPTO Assignment Center to see when the mark moved between entities.
  3. The parent company’s own filings — search Shiseido Company, Limited in SEC EDGAR full-text search for the acquisition disclosure.
  4. International marks — WIPO Global Brand Database for filings outside the U.S.
  5. Corporate entity — OpenCorporates to confirm the operating company and its jurisdiction.

Drunk Elephant ownership FAQ

Who owns Drunk Elephant?

Shiseido, the Japanese beauty company (TSE: 4911), has owned Drunk Elephant since October 2019, when it paid US$845 million for the brand.

Did the founder leave after the sale?

No - Tiffany Masterson remained as Chief Creative Officer and President after Shiseido acquired the company.

Where is the brand legally held?

Through Drunk Elephant LLC, now under Shiseido. Confirm the current trademark owner in USPTO TSDR.
