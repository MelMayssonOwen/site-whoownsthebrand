Who owns Costa Coffee?

Verified to public record

Costa Coffee is owned by The Coca-Cola Company (NYSE: KO). Coca-Cola announced the acquisition of Costa from UK hospitality group Whitbread on 31 August 2018 and completed it on 3 January 2019 for US$4.9 billion. Costa (legal entity Costa Limited) is Coca-Cola's coffee platform worldwide.

The ownership chain

Ownership is rarely one hop. Here is the evidence chain for Costa Coffee, from the entity that holds the trademark to the ultimate parent, with the record that establishes each link.

  1. Costa Limited
    Operating company / trademark holder (UK)
    Source: UK IPO / EUIPO / WIPO
  2. Whitbread PLC (1995-2019)
    Former owner; sold to Coca-Cola
    Source: Whitbread / Coca-Cola announcements
  3. The Coca-Cola Company
    Ultimate parent since 3 January 2019
    Source: Coca-Cola 10-K, SEC EDGAR

Note: Whitbread had owned Costa since 1995; shareholder pressure to separate its coffee business preceded the Coca-Cola sale.

Verify this ownership yourself (free)

You do not have to take our word for it. The ownership of Costa Coffee is a matter of public record. Check it in minutes:

  1. Trademark owner, search USPTO Trademark Search for “Costa Coffee”, then open the record in TSDR to see the current registered owner.
  2. Ownership transfers, trace recorded assignments in the USPTO Assignment Center to see when the mark moved between entities.
  3. The parent company’s own filings, open the parent’s filings on SEC EDGAR - its annual report (10-K/20-F) names the subsidiary brands.
  4. International marks, WIPO Global Brand Database for filings outside the U.S.
  5. Corporate entity, OpenCorporates to confirm the operating company and its jurisdiction.

Costa Coffee ownership FAQ

Who owns Costa Coffee?

The Coca-Cola Company (NYSE: KO) has owned Costa Coffee since 3 January 2019, when it completed a US$4.9 billion purchase from Whitbread.

Did Coca-Cola really buy a coffee chain?

Yes. Costa gave Coca-Cola a global hot-beverage/coffee platform, including retail stores and the Costa Express machine network.

Who owned Costa before Coca-Cola?

UK group Whitbread PLC owned Costa from 1995 until the 2019 sale.
