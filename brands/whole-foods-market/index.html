Who owns Whole Foods Market?

Verified to public record

Whole Foods Market is owned by Amazon (NASDAQ: AMZN). Amazon acquired the publicly traded Whole Foods Market, Inc. in 2017 for US$13.7 billion (announced June, closed August 2017). The grocery chain operates as an Amazon subsidiary; Amazon lists it among its businesses in SEC filings.

The ownership chain

Note: The deal was Amazon's largest acquisition at the time and marked its major move into physical grocery retail.

Verify this ownership yourself (free)

You do not have to take our word for it. The ownership of Whole Foods Market is a matter of public record. Check it in minutes:

  1. Trademark owner, search USPTO Trademark Search for “Whole Foods Market”, then open the record in TSDR to see the current registered owner.
  2. Ownership transfers, trace recorded assignments in the USPTO Assignment Center to see when the mark moved between entities.
  3. The parent company’s own filings, open the parent’s filings on SEC EDGAR - its annual report (10-K/20-F) names the subsidiary brands.
  4. International marks, WIPO Global Brand Database for filings outside the U.S.
  5. Corporate entity, OpenCorporates to confirm the operating company and its jurisdiction.

Whole Foods Market ownership FAQ

Who owns Whole Foods?

Amazon (NASDAQ: AMZN) has owned Whole Foods Market since August 2017, when it completed a US$13.7 billion acquisition.

Was Whole Foods a public company?

Yes. It traded on NASDAQ under WFM until Amazon bought it and took it private in 2017.

How do I verify Amazon's ownership?

Amazon's 10-K annual report on SEC EDGAR describes the Whole Foods business; the trademark registrant appears in USPTO TSDR.
