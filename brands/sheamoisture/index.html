Who owns SheaMoisture?

Verified to public record

SheaMoisture is owned by Unilever (LSE: ULVR / NYSE: UL). Unilever acquired SheaMoisture's parent, Sundial Brands LLC, in late 2017; financial terms were not disclosed. SheaMoisture continues to operate through the Sundial Brands entity under Unilever, which holds the trademark rights.

Verify this ownership yourself (free)

You do not have to take our word for it. The ownership of SheaMoisture is a matter of public record. Check it in minutes:

  1. Trademark owner — search USPTO Trademark Search for “SheaMoisture”, then open the record in TSDR to see the current registered owner.
  2. Ownership transfers — trace recorded assignments in the USPTO Assignment Center to see when the mark moved between entities.
  3. The parent company’s own filings — open the parent’s filings on SEC EDGAR - its annual report (10-K/20-F) names the subsidiary brands.
  4. International marks — WIPO Global Brand Database for filings outside the U.S.
  5. Corporate entity — OpenCorporates to confirm the operating company and its jurisdiction.

SheaMoisture ownership FAQ

Who owns SheaMoisture?

Unilever has owned SheaMoisture since acquiring Sundial Brands in late 2017. Deal terms were not disclosed.

Is SheaMoisture Black-owned?

It was founded and built as a Black-owned business, but corporate ownership passed to Unilever in 2017. The founder remains publicly associated with the brand and related ventures, but Unilever is the corporate owner.

What company legally holds the brand?

Sundial Brands LLC, now a Unilever subsidiary. Confirm the trademark registrant in USPTO TSDR.
