Who owns Ben & Jerry's?

Verified to public record

Ben & Jerry's is owned by The Magnum Ice Cream Company (Euronext Amsterdam / NYSE: MICC). Unilever bought Ben & Jerry's Homemade, Inc. in April 2000 for about US$326 million; in 2025 Unilever demerged its entire ice cream division, Ben & Jerry's included, into the newly listed Magnum Ice Cream Company. So the brand's ultimate parent changed from Unilever to Magnum on 1 July 2025.

Ben & Jerry's ownership FAQ

Is Ben & Jerry's still owned by Unilever?

No - not since 2025. Unilever owned Ben & Jerry's from 2000 to 2025, then spun its ice cream business (Ben & Jerry's, Magnum, Breyers, Wall's) into The Magnum Ice Cream Company, which became a separate listed company on 1 July 2025. Magnum is now the ultimate parent.

Does Ben & Jerry's operate independently?

Partly. The 2000 acquisition agreement gave Ben & Jerry's an independent board that controls its social mission and brand integrity, a structure that carried into the Magnum era. It is not a fully autonomous company - the trademark and equity sit with the parent.

Who is Ben & Jerry's actual legal/trademark owner?

The operating company is Ben & Jerry's Homemade, Inc., a Vermont corporation that holds the U.S. trademark registrations. You can confirm the current recorded owner in USPTO TSDR and the assignment history in the USPTO Assignment Center.
