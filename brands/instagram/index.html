Who owns Instagram?

Verified to public record

Instagram is owned by Meta Platforms, Inc. (NASDAQ: META). Facebook, Inc. acquired Instagram in 2012 for about US$1 billion (announced April, closed September 2012); Facebook renamed itself Meta Platforms in 2021. Instagram operates as Instagram, LLC under Meta.

The ownership chain

Ownership is rarely one hop. Here is the evidence chain for Instagram, from the entity that holds the trademark to the ultimate parent, with the record that establishes each link.

  1. Instagram, LLC
    Operating company / trademark holder
    Source: USPTO trademark register
  2. Kevin Systrom & Mike Krieger (to 2012)
    Founders; sold to Facebook
    Source: SEC filings / coverage
  3. Meta Platforms, Inc. (fmr. Facebook)
    Ultimate parent since 2012
    Source: Meta 10-K, SEC EDGAR

Note: The 2012 purchase is a landmark tech acquisition; Instagram had roughly a dozen employees at the time.

Verify this ownership yourself (free)

You do not have to take our word for it. The ownership of Instagram is a matter of public record. Check it in minutes:

  1. Trademark owner, search USPTO Trademark Search for “Instagram”, then open the record in TSDR to see the current registered owner.
  2. Ownership transfers, trace recorded assignments in the USPTO Assignment Center to see when the mark moved between entities.
  3. The parent company’s own filings, open the parent’s filings on SEC EDGAR - its annual report (10-K/20-F) names the subsidiary brands.
  4. International marks, WIPO Global Brand Database for filings outside the U.S.
  5. Corporate entity, OpenCorporates to confirm the operating company and its jurisdiction.

Instagram ownership FAQ

Who owns Instagram?

Meta Platforms, Inc. (NASDAQ: META) - the company formerly called Facebook, Inc. It has owned Instagram since 2012.

How much did Facebook pay for Instagram?

About US$1 billion in cash and stock, in a deal announced in April 2012 and completed that September.

Is Instagram the same company as Facebook?

Instagram is a separate legal entity (Instagram, LLC) but is wholly owned by Meta Platforms, the same parent that owns Facebook and WhatsApp.
