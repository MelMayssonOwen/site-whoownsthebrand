Who owns CeraVe?

Verified to public record

CeraVe is owned by L'Oreal (Euronext Paris: OR). L'Oreal bought CeraVe in 2017 for US$1.3 billion, acquiring it, along with AcneFree and Ambi, from Valeant Pharmaceuticals (now Bausch Health). CeraVe sits inside L'Oreal's Dermatological Beauty division and its trademarks are held by L'Oreal entities.

Verify this ownership yourself (free)

You do not have to take our word for it. The ownership of CeraVe is a matter of public record. Check it in minutes:

  1. Trademark owner, search USPTO Trademark Search for “CeraVe”, then open the record in TSDR to see the current registered owner.
  2. Ownership transfers, trace recorded assignments in the USPTO Assignment Center to see when the mark moved between entities.
  3. The parent company’s own filings, search L'Oreal S.A. in SEC EDGAR full-text search for the acquisition disclosure.
  4. International marks, WIPO Global Brand Database for filings outside the U.S.
  5. Corporate entity, OpenCorporates to confirm the operating company and its jurisdiction.

CeraVe ownership FAQ

Who owns CeraVe?

L'Oreal has owned CeraVe since 2017, when it bought the brand from Valeant/Bausch Health for US$1.3 billion (a package that also included AcneFree and Ambi).

Is CeraVe French?

The brand is American in origin (created 2005) but its owner, L'Oreal, is a French company listed in Paris.

How can I check who owns the CeraVe trademark?

Search 'CeraVe' in USPTO TSDR for the current registrant and check WIPO's Global Brand Database for international registrations.
