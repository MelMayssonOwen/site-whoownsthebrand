Brand-ownership glossary

The terms you need to read a trademark record and trace a brand to its ultimate parent, defined plainly.

Registrant / trademark owner of record
The legal entity listed as the current owner of a trademark registration in USPTO records. It is the starting point for ownership research, but not always the ultimate parent.
Assignor
The party transferring ownership of a trademark in an assignment.
Assignee
The party receiving ownership of a trademark in an assignment, i.e., the new owner.
Trademark assignment
A recorded transfer of trademark ownership from one entity to another. Public at the USPTO Assignment Center.
Operating company
The entity that actually runs a brand’s business day to day. It may or may not be the same entity that holds the trademark or the ultimate parent.
Holding company
A company that exists to own other companies or assets (including trademarks) rather than to operate a business itself. Alphabet Inc. is a holding company above Google.
Parent company
A company that owns a controlling interest in another company (a subsidiary).
Ultimate parent
The company at the top of the ownership chain that is not itself owned by another company. This is the answer to who really owns it.
Subsidiary
A company controlled by another company (its parent), usually through majority ownership.
Ultimate beneficial owner (UBO)
The natural person(s) who ultimately own or control an entity. Relevant for privately held brands where the chain ends in individuals rather than a public company.
DBA (doing business as)
A trade name a company operates under that differs from its registered legal name. A brand can be a DBA rather than a separate entity.
SEC EDGAR
The U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission’s public database of company filings. A public company’s 10-K or 20-F annual report lists the brands and subsidiaries it owns.
TSDR
Trademark Status and Document Retrieval, the USPTO system for viewing a specific registration’s live status, owner, and documents.
Madrid System / WIPO
The international trademark registration system administered by WIPO. The WIPO Global Brand Database is where you search international marks.
