Who owns Native?

Verified to public record

Native (Native deodorant) is owned by Procter & Gamble (NYSE: PG). P&G acquired the direct-to-consumer brand from founder Moiz Ali in late 2017 in a deal reported at about US$100 million. Native operates under P&G, and its trademark registrations sit with a P&G-controlled entity.

Verify this ownership yourself (free)

You do not have to take our word for it. The ownership of Native is a matter of public record. Check it in minutes:

  1. Trademark owner — search USPTO Trademark Search for “Native”, then open the record in TSDR to see the current registered owner.
  2. Ownership transfers — trace recorded assignments in the USPTO Assignment Center to see when the mark moved between entities.
  3. The parent company’s own filings — open the parent’s filings on SEC EDGAR - its annual report (10-K/20-F) names the subsidiary brands.
  4. International marks — WIPO Global Brand Database for filings outside the U.S.
  5. Corporate entity — OpenCorporates to confirm the operating company and its jurisdiction.

Tools The free registry method above is the authoritative way to confirm ownership. If you want it pre-assembled — the trademark record, operating entity, and parent chain resolved on one screen — a paid lookup tool such as Ethos (ethosscan.com) automates it. It is one option; the USPTO and SEC sources above are free and definitive.

Native ownership FAQ

Who owns Native deodorant?

Procter & Gamble has owned Native since it acquired the brand from founder Moiz Ali in late 2017, in a deal reported at about US$100 million.

Is Native an independent startup?

No longer. It began as an independent direct-to-consumer startup but has been a Procter & Gamble brand since 2017.

How do I confirm the current owner?

Search the 'Native' trademark in USPTO TSDR for the current registrant, then check the Assignment Center for the recorded transfer to P&G's entity.
