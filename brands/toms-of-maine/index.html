Who owns Tom's of Maine?

Verified to public record

Tom's of Maine is owned by Colgate-Palmolive (NYSE: CL). In March 2006 Colgate bought an 84% controlling stake in Tom's of Maine, Inc. for about US$100 million from founders Tom and Kate Chappell. The natural-products brand runs as a Colgate subsidiary, and Colgate lists it among its brands in SEC filings.

Verify this ownership yourself (free)

You do not have to take our word for it. The ownership of Tom's of Maine is a matter of public record. Check it in minutes:

  1. Trademark owner, search USPTO Trademark Search for “Tom's of Maine”, then open the record in TSDR to see the current registered owner.
  2. Ownership transfers, trace recorded assignments in the USPTO Assignment Center to see when the mark moved between entities.
  3. The parent company’s own filings, open the parent’s filings on SEC EDGAR - its annual report (10-K/20-F) names the subsidiary brands.
  4. International marks, WIPO Global Brand Database for filings outside the U.S.
  5. Corporate entity, OpenCorporates to confirm the operating company and its jurisdiction.

Tom's of Maine ownership FAQ

Who owns Tom's of Maine?

Colgate-Palmolive (NYSE: CL) has held a controlling stake since 2006, when it bought 84% of the company for about US$100 million. It operates as a Colgate subsidiary.

Is Tom's of Maine still family owned?

Not in control. Colgate holds the majority; the founding Chappell family kept a minority interest and a mission role, but the brand is controlled by Colgate.

Where is the trademark registered?

To Tom's of Maine, Inc. Confirm the current owner in USPTO TSDR and check assignment history in the USPTO Assignment Center.
