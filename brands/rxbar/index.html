Who owns RXBAR?

Verified to public record

RXBAR is owned by Kellanova (NYSE: K). Kellogg Company bought RXBAR's maker (then Chicago Bar Company) for US$600 million in October 2017. When Kellogg split in 2023, its global snacking business became Kellanova, which keeps RXBAR. The bars are produced by Insurgent Brands LLC, a Kellanova subsidiary.

The ownership chain

Ownership is rarely one hop. Here is the evidence chain for RXBAR, from the entity that holds the trademark to the ultimate parent, with the record that establishes each link.

  1. Insurgent Brands LLC (fmr. Chicago Bar Company)
    Operating company / trademark holder
    Source: USPTO trademark register
  2. Kellogg Company (2017-2023)
    Acquirer; split in 2023
    Source: Kellogg 8-K/10-K
  3. Kellanova
    Ultimate parent since 2023 split (snacks)
    Source: Kellanova 10-K, SEC EDGAR

Note: After the 2023 Kellogg separation, snacks went to Kellanova (NYSE: K) and North American cereal to WK Kellogg Co (NYSE: KLG). RXBAR is a snack, so it sits with Kellanova.

Verify this ownership yourself (free)

You do not have to take our word for it. The ownership of RXBAR is a matter of public record. Check it in minutes:

  1. Trademark owner, search USPTO Trademark Search for “RXBAR”, then open the record in TSDR to see the current registered owner.
  2. Ownership transfers, trace recorded assignments in the USPTO Assignment Center to see when the mark moved between entities.
  3. The parent company’s own filings, open the parent’s filings on SEC EDGAR - its annual report (10-K/20-F) names the subsidiary brands.
  4. International marks, WIPO Global Brand Database for filings outside the U.S.
  5. Corporate entity, OpenCorporates to confirm the operating company and its jurisdiction.

RXBAR ownership FAQ

Who owns RXBAR now?

Kellanova (NYSE: K), the snacking company created when Kellogg split in 2023. Kellogg originally bought RXBAR in 2017 for US$600 million.

Is RXBAR owned by Kellanova or WK Kellogg Co?

Kellanova. The 2023 split put snacks (including RXBAR) under Kellanova and North American cereal under WK Kellogg Co.

What legal entity makes RXBAR?

Insurgent Brands LLC, the renamed Chicago Bar Company, a Kellanova subsidiary. Confirm the trademark owner in USPTO TSDR.
