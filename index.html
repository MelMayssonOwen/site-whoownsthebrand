Who really owns that brand?

The trademark is registered to an LLC you have never heard of. Is that indie label secretly owned by a conglomerate? This is a free library that traces brands to their ultimate parent company, showing the full chain of evidence (trademark record → operating entity → ultimate parent, with dates) and citing the primary record for every claim.

How to find out who owns a brand, in one paragraph

Search the brand name in USPTO Trademark Search and open the record in TSDR to see the current registered owner. Trace ownership changes in the USPTO Assignment Center. If that owner is a subsidiary, find the ultimate parent in the company’s SEC EDGAR filings. Use WIPO for international marks and OpenCorporates to confirm the legal entity. Full step-by-step guide →
