Who owns Beats by Dre?

Verified to public record

Beats by Dre is owned by Apple (NASDAQ: AAPL). Apple acquired Beats Electronics, LLC (and Beats Music) in 2014 for US$3 billion — about US$2.6 billion cash plus US$400 million in stock (announced May, closed August 2014). Beats is Apple's largest acquisition to date and its headphone brand.

The ownership chain

Verify this ownership yourself (free)

You do not have to take our word for it. The ownership of Beats by Dre is a matter of public record. Check it in minutes:

  1. Trademark owner — search USPTO Trademark Search for “Beats by Dre”, then open the record in TSDR to see the current registered owner.
  2. Ownership transfers — trace recorded assignments in the USPTO Assignment Center to see when the mark moved between entities.
  3. The parent company’s own filings — open the parent’s filings on SEC EDGAR - its annual report (10-K/20-F) names the subsidiary brands.
  4. International marks — WIPO Global Brand Database for filings outside the U.S.
  5. Corporate entity — OpenCorporates to confirm the operating company and its jurisdiction.

Beats by Dre ownership FAQ

Who owns Beats headphones?

Apple (NASDAQ: AAPL) has owned Beats since 2014, when it acquired Beats Electronics for US$3 billion.

Does Dr. Dre still own Beats?

No. Dr. Dre and Jimmy Iovine sold Beats to Apple in 2014 and joined Apple; Apple owns the brand.

What is the legal entity behind Beats?

Beats Electronics, LLC, an Apple subsidiary. Confirm the current trademark owner in USPTO TSDR.
