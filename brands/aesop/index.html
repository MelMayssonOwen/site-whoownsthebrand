Who owns Aesop?

Verified to public record

Aesop is owned by L'Oreal (Euronext Paris: OR). L'Oreal agreed to buy Aesop from Brazil's Natura &Co in April 2023 and completed the deal in August 2023 for a reported ~US$2.5 billion, L'Oreal's largest-ever brand acquisition. Aesop had been fully owned by Natura &Co since 2016 (majority stake from 2012).

Verify this ownership yourself (free)

You do not have to take our word for it. The ownership of Aesop is a matter of public record. Check it in minutes:

  1. Trademark owner — search USPTO Trademark Search for “Aesop”, then open the record in TSDR to see the current registered owner.
  2. Ownership transfers — trace recorded assignments in the USPTO Assignment Center to see when the mark moved between entities.
  3. The parent company’s own filings — search L'Oreal S.A. in SEC EDGAR full-text search for the acquisition disclosure.
  4. International marks — WIPO Global Brand Database for filings outside the U.S.
  5. Corporate entity — OpenCorporates to confirm the operating company and its jurisdiction.

Aesop ownership FAQ

Who owns Aesop?

L'Oreal has owned Aesop since August 2023, when it completed its ~US$2.5 billion purchase from Natura &Co.

Was Aesop owned by Natura?

Yes. Brazil's Natura &Co took a majority stake in 2012, full ownership by 2016, and sold Aesop to L'Oreal in 2023.

How much did L'Oreal pay for Aesop?

Reports put the deal around US$2.5 billion; Natura cited an enterprise value near A$3.7 billion. Treat the exact figure as reported rather than officially fixed.
