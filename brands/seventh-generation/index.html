Who owns Seventh Generation?

Verified to public record

Seventh Generation is owned by Unilever (LSE: ULVR / NYSE: UL). Unilever acquired Seventh Generation, Inc. in September 2016; the deal terms were not officially disclosed, though press reports put it around US$600-700 million. The eco-cleaning brand is a Unilever subsidiary and appears among Unilever's brands in company disclosures.

Verify this ownership yourself (free)

You do not have to take our word for it. The ownership of Seventh Generation is a matter of public record. Check it in minutes:

  1. Trademark owner — search USPTO Trademark Search for “Seventh Generation”, then open the record in TSDR to see the current registered owner.
  2. Ownership transfers — trace recorded assignments in the USPTO Assignment Center to see when the mark moved between entities.
  3. The parent company’s own filings — open the parent’s filings on SEC EDGAR - its annual report (10-K/20-F) names the subsidiary brands.
  4. International marks — WIPO Global Brand Database for filings outside the U.S.
  5. Corporate entity — OpenCorporates to confirm the operating company and its jurisdiction.

Seventh Generation ownership FAQ

Who owns Seventh Generation?

Unilever has owned it since September 2016. It is a Unilever subsidiary in the home & personal care portfolio.

Did Seventh Generation go to the Magnum ice-cream spinoff?

No. Only Unilever's ice cream brands (Ben & Jerry's, Magnum, Breyers, Wall's) moved to The Magnum Ice Cream Company in 2025. Seventh Generation is a cleaning/personal-care brand and stayed with Unilever.

How much did Unilever pay?

Unilever did not disclose official terms; trade press reported roughly US$600-700 million. Treat the exact figure as unconfirmed.
