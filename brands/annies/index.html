Who owns Annie's?

Verified to public record

Annie's (Annie's Homegrown) is owned by General Mills (NYSE: GIS). General Mills acquired the publicly traded Annie's, Inc. in 2014 for about US$820 million (roughly US$46 per share). The organic mac-and-cheese and snacks brand is a General Mills subsidiary and appears in its brand portfolio disclosures.

Verify this ownership yourself (free)

You do not have to take our word for it. The ownership of Annie's is a matter of public record. Check it in minutes:

  1. Trademark owner, search USPTO Trademark Search for “Annie's”, then open the record in TSDR to see the current registered owner.
  2. Ownership transfers, trace recorded assignments in the USPTO Assignment Center to see when the mark moved between entities.
  3. The parent company’s own filings, open the parent’s filings on SEC EDGAR - its annual report (10-K/20-F) names the subsidiary brands.
  4. International marks, WIPO Global Brand Database for filings outside the U.S.
  5. Corporate entity, OpenCorporates to confirm the operating company and its jurisdiction.

Annie's ownership FAQ

Who owns Annie's?

General Mills (NYSE: GIS) has owned Annie's since 2014, when it bought the company for about US$820 million.

Wasn't Annie's its own public company?

Yes. Annie's IPO'd on the NYSE (ticker BNNY) in 2012, then General Mills acquired it and took it private in 2014.

How do I verify the trademark owner?

Search 'Annie's Homegrown' in USPTO TSDR and review the assignment record for the transfer into General Mills' structure.
