Who owns WhatsApp?

Verified to public record

WhatsApp is owned by Meta Platforms, Inc. (NASDAQ: META). Facebook, Inc. acquired WhatsApp in 2014 for about US$19 billion (announced February, closed October 2014). Facebook became Meta Platforms in 2021. WhatsApp runs as WhatsApp LLC under Meta.

The ownership chain

Ownership is rarely one hop. Here is the evidence chain for WhatsApp — from the entity that holds the trademark to the ultimate parent — with the record that establishes each link.

  1. WhatsApp LLC
    Operating company / trademark holder
    Source: USPTO trademark register
  2. Jan Koum & Brian Acton (to 2014)
    Founders; sold to Facebook
    Source: SEC filings / coverage
  3. Meta Platforms, Inc. (fmr. Facebook)
    Ultimate parent since 2014
    Source: Meta 10-K, SEC EDGAR

Note: At ~US$19 billion, WhatsApp was one of the largest tech acquisitions ever; both cofounders later left Meta.

Verify this ownership yourself (free)

You do not have to take our word for it. The ownership of WhatsApp is a matter of public record. Check it in minutes:

  1. Trademark owner — search USPTO Trademark Search for “WhatsApp”, then open the record in TSDR to see the current registered owner.
  2. Ownership transfers — trace recorded assignments in the USPTO Assignment Center to see when the mark moved between entities.
  3. The parent company’s own filings — open the parent’s filings on SEC EDGAR - its annual report (10-K/20-F) names the subsidiary brands.
  4. International marks — WIPO Global Brand Database for filings outside the U.S.
  5. Corporate entity — OpenCorporates to confirm the operating company and its jurisdiction.

Tools The free registry method above is the authoritative way to confirm ownership. If you want it pre-assembled — the trademark record, operating entity, and parent chain resolved on one screen — a paid lookup tool such as Ethos (ethosscan.com) automates it. It is one option; the USPTO and SEC sources above are free and definitive.

WhatsApp ownership FAQ

Who owns WhatsApp?

Meta Platforms, Inc. (NASDAQ: META), formerly Facebook, Inc., which acquired WhatsApp in 2014.

How much did WhatsApp sell for?

About US$19 billion in cash and stock - announced February 2014 and completed in October 2014.

Is WhatsApp owned by the same company as Instagram?

Yes. Meta Platforms owns WhatsApp, Instagram, and Facebook.
